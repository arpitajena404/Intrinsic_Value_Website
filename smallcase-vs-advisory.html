Why Standardized Equity Baskets Can Drain Capital (Tax Churn)

Standardized stock baskets and Smallcases require automatic rebalancing alerts. When you rebalance, you execute multiple buy/sell transactions simultaneously. In India, every sell trade of a stock held for under a year attracts a 20% Short Term Capital Gains (STCG) tax. Over a year, this churn eats significantly into your compounding compound interest, reducing actual net returns.

The Power of Cyclical Sector Rotation for Multibagger Growth

Rather than holding a static list of stocks, our investment advisor framework cycles capital out of matured/overvalued sectors into sectors entering structural economic upcycles. By analyzing promoter buying behavior and sector valuation indices, we identify turnaround multibagger opportunities with a high margin of safety.

Personalized Research Advisory vs. Automated Copy Trading

A Smallcase is essentially a copy-trade mechanism. If a stock in a Smallcase has run up 100% since its inclusion, a new subscriber is still forced to buy it at peak valuation to complete the basket structure. With Intrinsic Value, we customize entry points so that you never buy a stock after it has lost its margin of safety.
