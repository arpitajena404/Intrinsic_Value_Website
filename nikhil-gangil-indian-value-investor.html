Nikhil Gangil

Indian Value Investor & Cyclical Investing Specialist

Nikhil Gangil is an Indian value investor and the Founder of Intrinsic Value Equity Advisors, with over 13 years of experience in Indian equity markets. He is known for his focus on long-term value investing, cyclical opportunities, and disciplined capital protection.

His work combines business quality analysis, valuation discipline, and an understanding of economic and sectoral cycles, with the objective of achieving sustainable long-term compounding while avoiding permanent capital loss.

Nikhil Gangil is frequently cited by Indian financial media for his views on market cycles, valuation discipline, and long-term investing in Indian equities.

Investment Philosophy

Nikhil’s investing philosophy is built on the belief that value and growth are not opposites, but complements. He emphasizes that long-term wealth creation comes from buying well-run businesses at reasonable valuations and allowing time, process, and discipline to work in the investor’s favor.

His approach prioritizes downside risk management first, with returns viewed as a by-product of correct decision-making rather than speculation.
